## Non-goals

Career Alpha is not trying to become:

- a fake-experience generator;
- a generic resume template marketplace;
- a job-board scraper with no evidence layer;
- a leaderboard that pretends one career path fits everyone;
- a system that automatically turns weak evidence into strong claims.

The product thesis remains:

> **Find the missing fact with the highest career leverage, build it honestly, and let real market feedback update the next move.**
