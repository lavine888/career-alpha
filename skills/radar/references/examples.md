# Radar Examples

These examples are about **decision quality**, not forecasts.

## Example 1 — Generic RAG

### Weak analysis

> RAG is still hot because many companies use it.

Problem: this measures adoption, not marginal career advantage.

### Better analysis

```text
Direction: Generic RAG application development
Demand: high
Talent scarcity: low
Proofability: high
Tutorial saturation: extreme
Verdict: CONSENSUS / SATURATED as a standalone differentiator

Interpretation:
RAG remains useful as a baseline skill, but a generic PDF-chat project does not create much Career Alpha. A stronger wedge may exist in retrieval evaluation, domain-specific data quality, latency/cost optimization, or production observability.
```

## Example 2 — Agent Evaluation

```text
Direction: Agent Evaluation
Demand momentum: high
Talent scarcity: medium-high
Proofability: high
Timing: early-to-growing
Verdict: EARLY / GROWING

Why interesting:
- non-deterministic agents require repeatable evaluation;
- production teams need failure taxonomies and regression checks;
- candidates can create benchmark evidence without production access.

Counter-evidence:
- many teams may keep eval responsibilities inside general agent engineering roles;
- dedicated titles may remain rare.

Career implication:
Do not optimize for the title “Agent Eval Engineer”. Build evaluation evidence that improves an Agent Engineer profile.
```

## Example 3 — A trend that is too early

```text
Direction: fully autonomous multi-agent company operating systems
Attention: high
Direct hiring evidence: low
Production evidence: limited
Proofability: medium
Verdict: TOO_EARLY / HYPE
```

The correct recommendation may be: learn enough to monitor it, but do not spend the next month building your entire portfolio around it.

## Example 4 — Candidate-specific fit

The same trend should score differently for two users.

### Candidate A

- backend engineer;
- distributed systems experience;
- wants AI infra.

Agent observability may have high personal fit.

### Candidate B

- growth marketer;
- no coding background;
- strong creator distribution.

Agent observability may be a bad personal wedge even if the market trend is real. AI-native growth operations or GEO may provide a more accessible proof path.

`/radar` must never confuse **market attractiveness** with **personal opportunity**.
