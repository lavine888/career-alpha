# Positioning Channel Templates

The same evidence should be translated differently by channel.

## 1. Resume summary

Goal: 2–4 lines, high signal density.

Structure:

```text
Current role / background + target direction
1–2 strongest evidence-backed differentiators
Optional domain / system context
```

Avoid:

- long adjective stacks;
- unsupported titles;
- generic “passionate / hardworking / fast learner”.

## 2. Resume bullet

Preferred pattern:

```text
Action → System / problem → Evidence / result → Boundary when needed
```

Examples:

```text
Built a 50-task reliability benchmark for a tool-using agent, defining success criteria, failure taxonomy and reproducible baseline / harness comparisons.
```

```text
Submitted and iterated on an upstream PR adding X, responding to maintainer review and validating the change with Y tests.
```

## 3. 30-second introduction

Structure:

```text
Who I am now
What problem family I keep working on
One concrete proof
What kind of team / role I am looking for
```

Keep it conversational. Do not read the resume aloud.

## 4. HR / recruiter opener

Target length: roughly 80–160 Chinese characters or similarly concise English.

Structure:

```text
identity / target
one relevant proof
why this role / company
light CTA
```

Recruiters do not need architecture details in the first message.

## 5. Hiring manager / founder DM

Structure:

```text
Specific observation about their problem / product
Relevant proof you already built
Why your proof maps to their problem
Offer a concrete artifact / small reproduction / conversation
```

Weak:

> I am very interested in AI and hope to join your company.

Stronger:

> I noticed your team is pushing tool-using agents into production. I've been benchmarking retry, recovery and context policies on a fixed task suite and documenting failure modes. If eval / reliability is becoming a bottleneck for your team, I can share the benchmark or reproduce a smaller version around your workflow.

## 6. GitHub / portfolio project intro

Structure:

```text
Problem
Why it matters now
What was built
What was measured
What failed / limitation
How to reproduce
```

This channel can be more technical than recruiter outreach.

## 7. Behavioral interview story

Use a compact STAR / decision structure:

```text
Situation
Constraint
Decision
Action
Result
What changed after learning
```

Do not inflate outcome if evidence is weak.

## 8. Channel consistency

All channel versions must map back to the same Claim–Evidence Ledger.

It is fine for emphasis to change.

It is not fine for:

- recruiter message to claim `led` while resume says `contributed`;
- LinkedIn to say production while evidence says prototype;
- interview story to add a number that is absent from the ledger.
