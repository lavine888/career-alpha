# Source Pack — AI Product / Applied AI PM

Use this pack when `/radar` or `/wedge` evaluates AI Product Manager, Applied AI, workflow product, AI operations or product-engineering hybrid opportunities.

## Demand signals

Prioritize:

1. Product / PM job descriptions that explicitly mention AI workflows, model evaluation, automation, human-in-the-loop or agentic products.
2. Product releases where the hard problem is workflow reliability, adoption or outcome quality rather than model access.
3. Case studies with real user behavior, support burden, fallback design, retention or business outcomes.
4. Founder / product-team writing about implementation bottlenecks and operational trade-offs.
5. Engineering + product collaboration patterns around evals, guardrails, permissions, data and feedback loops.

## Useful capability clusters

- Workflow decomposition
- Evaluation design for product quality
- Human fallback / escalation
- AI UX and trust calibration
- Product instrumentation and outcome metrics
- Agent permission / action design
- AI feature economics and cost-to-serve
- Enterprise implementation / change management
- Technical prototyping for PMs

## Saturation checks

Downgrade directions when:

- the candidate output is only a PRD with no prototype or user evidence;
- the role is generic PM with “AI” added to the title;
- the project measures clicks but not task success or user outcomes;
- there is no explicit failure handling or evaluation plan.

## High-value proof

Prefer:

- working workflow prototype;
- user-task map and failure map;
- evaluation rubric tied to product outcomes;
- human fallback policy;
- before/after process metric;
- user interviews or operator feedback;
- evidence that the candidate made a product trade-off, not only wrote requirements.

## Example wedge questions

- Where does AI change the workflow rather than merely add a chatbot?
- What outcome is expensive enough that a team will pay for better reliability?
- Which PMs can actually design evals and reason about model failure?
- What proof would distinguish an AI-native PM from a generic PM using AI tools?
