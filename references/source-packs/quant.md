# Source Pack — Quant Research / Systematic Trading

Use this pack when `/radar` or `/wedge` evaluates quant research, systematic trading, portfolio research, alternative data or research-engineering opportunities.

## Demand signals

Prioritize:

1. First-party quant / research / trading job descriptions and team pages.
2. Research engineering requirements around data quality, reproducibility, backtesting, execution and model risk.
3. Public research notes, technical blogs or conference material describing current methodology and infrastructure problems.
4. New datasets, market-structure changes or tooling that create research opportunities.
5. Repeated demand for hybrid profiles spanning statistics, engineering and market reasoning.

## Useful capability clusters

- Research reproducibility
- Backtest robustness
- Transaction-cost and slippage modeling
- Factor validation and decay analysis
- Alternative-data pipelines
- Portfolio construction and risk
- Market microstructure
- Research tooling / experiment infrastructure
- Regime and failure analysis

## Saturation checks

Downgrade directions when:

- the project reports CAGR or Sharpe without realistic costs;
- it relies on one favorable period or one parameter setting;
- there is no out-of-sample or sensitivity analysis;
- the “alpha” is a common textbook factor with no new evidence;
- the result cannot be reproduced from saved data and code.

## High-value proof

Prefer:

- reproducible research notebook / pipeline;
- explicit train / validation / test or walk-forward design;
- transaction cost model;
- parameter sensitivity;
- failure regimes and drawdown analysis;
- baseline comparison;
- clear distinction between research hypothesis and production strategy.

## Example wedge questions

- Which research bottleneck is caused by data / tooling rather than lack of another model?
- What can be measured robustly within a small public-data project?
- Which capability is under-supplied because it requires both statistics and engineering?
- What result still survives after costs, sensitivity and regime checks?
