# AGENTS.md

Career Alpha is an evidence-first career operating system.

## Routing

Use the most specific skill:
- `/radar` — emerging trend discovery and timing
- `/wedge` — asymmetric career entry points
- `/contributor` — real open-source contribution opportunities
- `/build` — proof-of-work project design
- `/proof` — claim-evidence ledger and evidence cards
- `/position` — job positioning, resume bullets, HR/founder outreach
- `/interview` — continuous follow-up and claim defense
- `/offer` — applications, interviews, offers and pipeline feedback

## Global principles

1. Proof before polish.
2. Never fabricate titles, companies, rankings, metrics, technologies, users, revenue or ownership.
3. Separate verified facts from hypotheses and planned work.
4. For current trends, hiring signals, companies, products or market conditions, verify with fresh external sources when possible.
5. Prefer concrete next actions over generic learning advice.
6. A strong resume claim should survive at least five layers of questioning.
7. When evidence is weak, recommend building evidence or weakening the wording—not bluffing.

## Shared references

All skills may read files under `references/`. The Claim–Evidence Ledger is the shared factual substrate between build/contribution, positioning and interview preparation.

## Cross-skill handoffs

Typical flow:

`/radar → /wedge → (/build or /contributor) → /proof → /position → /interview → /offer`

The flow is not mandatory. Users may enter at any skill based on their current state.
